Frequently Asked Questions

How do I use the Crop Yield Prediction?
Go to the "Crop Yield Prediction" page, enter your soil and weather details, and click "Recommend Crop" to get suggestions.
How can I submit feedback?
Visit the "Farmer Feedback" page, fill in the form, and submit your feedback. You can also use voice input if your browser supports it.
How do I download reports?
On the Feedback and Prediction pages, use the "Download Report" buttons to get CSV files of your data.
How do I change the website language?
Use the language dropdown on each page to select your preferred language.
Who can I contact for support?
For technical support, please use the feedback form or contact the site administrator.

Help & Troubleshooting

  • If you have trouble with voice input, check your browser's microphone permissions.
  • For best experience, use the latest version of Chrome, Firefox, or Edge.
  • If you encounter errors, try refreshing the page or clearing your browser cache.
