Introduction

This website demonstrates the use of machine learning for predicting crop yields and incorporating NLP-based insights from textual data such as farmer reports or weather descriptions.

Features

  • Dashboard & Analytics: Visualize feedback and crop prediction trends.
  • Crop Yield Prediction: Input soil and weather data to find the best crop.
  • Farmer Feedback: Submit feedback and reports from the field.
  • Smart Agriculture Advisor: Analyze feedback and reports to get suggestions.
  • Crop Doctor: Upload plant images to diagnose diseases.
  • Crop Prices: View current market prices for crops.
  • NLP-Based Insight: Analyze text for insights.
  • FAQ & Help: Frequently asked questions and troubleshooting.
